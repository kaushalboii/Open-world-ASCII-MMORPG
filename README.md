## 📖 The Story & World Lore: The Genesis Fracture

> *"In the dawn before time had a name, existence was unified in the **Genesis Core**, an omni-elemental singularity pulsing with the **21 Primal Weaves**.*
>
> *Then came the **Great Fracture**. The Core ruptured, shattering geometry and uncoiling reality into **The Boundless Spiral** — an infinite ascending labyrinth of fractal chambers where physical laws warp with every ascending stratum.*
>
> *You awaken as **Kaiden the Spiral Wanderer**, born at the zero-coordinate of the Fracture. Attuned to the Primal Resonance through your glowing **Fractal Visor** and flowing **Flux Ribbon Scarf**, you possess the rare capacity to channel all 21 elemental disciplines.*
>
> *There is no ceiling to the Spiral. Step into the **Celestial Ascension Gates**, ascend through infinite chambers, purge rogue elemental entities, conquer the titanic **Colossi Boss Trials** every fifth floor, and synthesize the fractured elements into absolute equilibrium."*

## 🌀 Unlimited Level Engine (Floors 1 to $\infty$)

The game features a true procedural generation engine designed for **unlimited ascension**:
- **Continuous Biome Cycling**: Every chamber transitions across 10 distinct elemental realms (Ignis Forge, Glacial Labyrinth, Fulgur Bastion, Verdant Cradle, Abyssal Cistern, Umbral Void, Celestial Apex, Solstice Basin, Quantum Rift, and Chaos Core).
- **Procedurally Seeded Platforms**: Floor geometry, obstacle coordinates, and supply drops are mathematically generated based on floor indices.
- **Dynamic Weather VFX**: Ambient elemental particles (sparks, embers, frost crystals, bio-spores, void glyphs) drift through each biome.
- **Colossi Boss Trials**: Every 5th floor unleashes an Ancient Colossus (Pyroclast Titan, Glacial Archon, Tempest Colossus, Verdant Behemoth, Abyssal Leviathan, and more) boasting colossal health pools and unique attack phases.
- **Celestial Ascension Gates**: Eliminating all hostiles in any chamber awakens the shimmering portal on the far right. Stepping through ascends Kaiden to `Floor N + 1`, grants massive shard bounties, and saves your high-water mark in local storage.

## 🌟 Key Gameplay Systems

### 1. Martial Arts & Aerocast Plunge
- **Straight Punch & Cyan Blast**: High-speed kinetic martial strike accompanied by bright cyan shockwave bursts (`#00f0ff`).
- **High-Altitude Dropkick Stomp**: Leap from above onto hostiles or crates to crush them for **45+ kinetic damage**, rebounding high into the air.
- **Lucky Relic Blocks (`[ ? ]`)**: Strike suspended ancient relic blocks from below to release showers of Aether Shards, XP essence, and restorative Life Dew.
- **Aether Surge Star**: Rare golden celestial stars cloak Kaiden in a luminous prismatic aura, granting temporary **complete invulnerability** to vaporize enemies on contact.

### 2. Milestone Weapon Evolution (`Q` to Switch)
As you ascend the Spiral, new weapons unlock at milestone chambers:

| Weapon | Unlock Requirement | Attack Profile | Energy Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Martial Arts & Dagger** | Starter Weapon | Rapid flurry punches with cyan energy shockwaves and piercing stabs | 6 |
| **Broadsword** | Ascend to Floor 2 | Sweeping 3-stroke heavy blade combo unleashing wide crescent slash arcs | 12 |
| **Aether Blaster** | Ascend to Floor 4 | Long-range focused photon projectile beams crossing the screen | 10 |
| **Concussion Mortar** | Ascend to Floor 7 | High-yield ballistic mortar shells with wide-area kinetic detonations | 25 |
| **Tempest Wrath** | Ascend to Floor 10 | Screen-clearing high-voltage celestial lightning storm chain-zapping all hostiles | 30 |

### 3. Primal Forge & Talent Upgrades (`U` or `TAB` or `⚡ PRIMAL FORGE`)
Spend extracted Aether Shards to upgrade your hero:
- **Resonance Multiplier**: +30% attack damage to all martial strikes and weapons.
- **Magnetic Flux**: Increases shard pickup pull radius by +40px.
- **Genesis Vitality**: +30 Max HP and immediate health restoration.
- **Flux Capacitor**: +25 Max Energy for sustained combos and abilities.
- **Vortex Stride**: Grants extra mid-air jumps and +20% movement speed.
- **Toxic Flasks Refill**: Restocks corrosive elemental poison flasks.

### 4. Interactive NPCs & Sanctuary Sheep
- **Aria the Chronicler (`Ω`)**: Keeper of the Genesis Codex. Grants tactical intelligence and shard bounties.
- **Kaelen the Forge Master (`Ψ`)**: Ancient artisan residing on mid-spiral platforms, offering weapon refinements.
- **Seer Vesper (`Φ`)**: Astral oracle at milestone threshold chambers granting full restoration.
- **Sanctuary Sheep (`{m}`)**: Roaming pasture fauna in calm chambers. Pet them (`[E]`) to receive gentle bleats and `+25 XP` rewards!
- **Smashable Supply Crates (`[x]`) & Relic Chests (`[=]`)**: Break with weapons or stomp from above to extract hidden loot.

## 🧬 Sprite Fusion Rig Explorer & 3D IK Visualizer

The interactive Rig Explorer (`🧬 SPRITE ANIMATOR & RIG`) provides live inspection of the player character rig:
- **Hero Palette**: Dark navy suit (`#384873` / `#192138`), glowing cyan visor (`#00f0ff`), white sneakers (`#ffffff`), and flowing hot-pink scarf (`#ff2a85` / `#f53cd1`).
- **4 Animation States**: `🏃 RUN` (6-frame sprint), `🧘 IDLE` (4-frame breathing cycle), `🦘 JUMP` (3-frame apex arc), and `🥊 ATTACK` (Straight Punch).
- **Motion Prompt Input**: Live prompt evaluation (e.g., `"Straight punch animation"`).
- **4 Real-Time Renderers**:
  1. `🎨 PIXEL ART`: High-contrast procedural pixel sprite renderer.
  2. `🔤 SMOOTH ASCII`: Fluid 60 FPS ASCII character matrix with brightness mapping.
  3. `⚡ HYBRID`: Simultaneous pixel body with overlaid dynamic ASCII energy field.
  4. `🦴 3D SKELETON`: Raw joint bones and inverse kinematics angle links.
- **Interactive 3D Controls**: Live sliders for 3D Yaw (Turn), Spine Lean, Hip Crouch, and Auto-Spin.

## 🏗️ Technical Architecture

This game runs completely in the browser with **zero external image or audio assets** — every character pose, particle effect, and sound effect is synthesized via code.

```
Open-world-ASCII-MMORPG/
├── index.html                   # Stage canvas, top navigation, studio controls, mobile virtual pad
├── package.json                 # Project configuration (Vite, pure ES modules)
├── vite.config.js               # GitHub Pages relative build configuration
├── src/
│   ├── main.js                  # Central game loop, screen state machine & HUD
│   ├── engine/
│   │   ├── GameRegistry.js      # Central export registry linking all systems
│   │   ├── Input.js             # Keyboard, mouse, and mobile virtual touch buffering
│   │   ├── Camera.js            # Screen shake and coordinate transformations
│   │   ├── Renderer.js          # Canvas rendering pipelines
│   │   └── AsciiGrid.js         # Glyph matrix overlay
│   ├── audio/
│   │   └── SoundSynth.js        # Procedural Web Audio API synthesizer & chiptune BGM
│   ├── combat/
│   │   ├── DamageText.js        # Floating combat numbers and critical indicators
│   │   ├── SlashArc.js          # Animated crescent blade trails
│   │   └── AsciiFeedback.js     # Hit sparks and impact feedback
│   ├── entities/
│   │   ├── NPC.js               # Interactive story quest givers (Aria, Kaelen, Seer Vesper)
│   │   ├── Boss.js              # Multi-phase titan boss AI
│   │   ├── PlatformEnemy.js     # Elemental automata behaviors
│   │   └── EntityManager.js     # Entity tracking and collision
│   ├── ui/
│   │   ├── DialogueBox.js       # Retro typewriter narrative dialog system
│   │   └── MiniMap.js           # Real-time tactical radar scanner
│   ├── world/
│   │   ├── Destructibles.js     # Smashable supply crates, barrels, and relic chests
│   │   ├── Sheep.js             # Sanctuary pasture sheep AI
│   │   └── WorldMap.js          # World and terrace representations
│   ├── vfx/
```
