## 🕹️ Mario & Shooting Game Evolution

### 1. Mario-Style Jump & Stomp Mechanics
- **Enemy Head Stomp**: Fall onto enemies from above to squish them for 45+ damage, launch high into the air, and reset your air jumps!
- **Mystery Lucky Blocks (`[ ? ]`)**: Hit floating blocks from below with a headbutt to shower lucky gold coins and XP!
- **Power Star Mode**: Rare star drops grant temporary Mario-style rainbow invulnerability to crush all enemies on touch!

### 2. Enemy Loot Drops & Magnet System
- Defeated enemies drop bouncy **Gold Coins** (`🪙`), **XP Orbs** (`⚡`), **Heart Medkits** (`❤️`), and **Power Stars**!
- Drops bounce naturally on platforms and floor.
- **Loot Magnet**: Walk near drops (or upgrade magnet radius) to pull all loot towards you automatically!

### 3. Arsenal Evolution & In-Game Upgrade Shop (`U` or `TAB`)
Earn Gold Coins from kills to evolve your weapons and purchase power-up perks:
- **Combat Dagger / Knife (`melee/dagger-flurry.js`)**: 6-hit rapid flurry stabs with spark bursts (ultra-fast attack rate, low stamina).
- **Cyber Katana / Sword (`moves/sword-combo.js`)**: Wide 3-stroke cleaving combo with contact freeze frames and camera shake.
- **Laser Blaster / Pistol (`moves/pistol-shots.js`)**: Ranged run-and-gun shooting! Fires 3 aimed laser projectiles crossing the screen with weapon kick.
- **Rocket Bazooka (`moves/rocket-launcher.js`)**: Heavy shoulder launcher firing explosive missiles with screen-wide area-of-effect blast.
- **Celestial Lightning (`moves/lightning-call.js`)**: Summons screen-clearing lightning storm that chain-zaps all enemies.
- **Press `Q` to switch equipped weapons anytime in battle!**

### 4. Upgradable Power-Up Perks
- **Damage Overcharge**: +30% attack damage to all weapons.
- **Loot Magnet**: Increases pickup pull radius by +40px.
- **Heart Tank**: +30 Max HP and full health restoration.
- **Stamina Core**: +25 Max Energy for sustained combos and shooting.
- **Hermes Boots**: Grants Triple Jump and +20% move speed.
- **Poison Flasks Refill**: Restocks Hugo Duprez toxic gas clouds.

## 🧪 Interactive PixelFX Studio & Rig Explorer
- **VFX Studio Tab**: Run, inspect, and tweak all 244 effects from `patch-gg/pixelfx` across 21 categories with slow-mo (0.25x), direction flip, and backdrop selector.
- **Rig Explorer Tab**: Interactive 3D skeletal inverse kinematics (IK) visualizer with real-time sliders for Yaw, Lean, Crouch, Hand Reach, and Weapon Angle.
