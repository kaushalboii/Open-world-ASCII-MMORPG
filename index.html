† TINY GLYPHS †

An Open-World ASCII Action Adventure
[ CONTROLS ] A / D or Arrows: Run | W / Space: Jump (supports Double Jump!) | J / Left Click: Straight Punch | K / E / Right Click: Poison Flask (Sprite Fusion VFX) | R: Restart
Run through the cyberpunk ruins, double-jump across girders, unleash punch combos, and dissolve mutants in toxic gas clouds!
